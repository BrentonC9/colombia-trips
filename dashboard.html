Our Story

Colombia Trips Co. was born from a simple idea — Americans deserve a trusted, all-in-one resource to experience Colombia the right way. Not a travel blog. Not a booking site. A real local network.

How We Started

It started with
one trip

Colombia Trips Co. was created by two Americans who saw a massive gap in the market — millions of people curious about Colombia but held back by outdated perceptions and zero trusted resources built specifically for them.

Our co-founder Chris has spent years traveling to Colombia — 4 to 5 trips a year — building real personal relationships with hotel owners, restaurant operators, doctors, and local guides. That network is the foundation of everything we offer.

We're not a tech company that built a travel app. We're people who love Colombia, who know Colombia, and who wanted to share it with every American who's been curious but never took the leap.

Colombia has one of the most unfair reputations in travel. The reality on the ground — the people, the food, the landscapes, the culture — is so far removed from what most Americans have been told that it consistently shocks first-time visitors.
